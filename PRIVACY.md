# SlowLight Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

SlowLight is provided by Bobox Studio. This policy describes the Android app with package name com.nuks.slowlight.

## Data collection and sharing

SlowLight does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, health data, device identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports. It has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or internet permission. Breathing sessions are processed on your device.

## Settings stored on your device

The app stores preferences such as session length, light colour, brightness, reduced motion, and setup guidance locally using Android DataStore. These settings are not sent to Bobox Studio. They remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. App backup is disabled.

## Camera permission

Optional rear-torch mode requests camera permission solely to control the phone's flashlight. SlowLight does not take photographs, record video or audio, or store camera images. You can use screen mode without camera permission and revoke camera permission in Android settings at any time.

## Purchases

Google Play handles payment for the app under Google's own privacy policy and terms. SlowLight does not receive or store your payment-card or bank-account details.

## Support

If you contact support@boboxstudio.co.nz, Bobox Studio receives the information you choose to include in your message and uses it to respond to your request. Please do not send medical records or other sensitive information. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address, subject to any applicable record-retention requirements.

## Intended audience

SlowLight is intended for adults as a relaxation and visual breathing tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat health conditions.

## Changes and contact

We will update this policy before changes to the app's data practices take effect. Contact Bobox Studio at support@boboxstudio.co.nz with privacy questions.
